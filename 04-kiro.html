SpecsR1–R7 in EARS form, written before the code. R3.3 is the rule that ambiguous dates get flagged.
SteeringLocal by default. Show uncertainty. No silent date guessing. One page only.
HooksTests on every source edit; a parser review whenever the extraction file changes.
AgentsA privacy auditor that looks for anything moving fax content off-device.
MCPTwo tools, so date behaviour is checkable inside the agent loop.
PBTfast-check properties over the date and phone normalisers.
Committed on purpose — the AI tooling is not hidden from the repo.
