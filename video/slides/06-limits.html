05 · What it does not do

Small on purpose.

  • Page one only.Multi-page filings need fields joined across pages.
  • English only.Hindi and Marathi are the next language packs.
  • No storage.Close the tab and the record is gone — that is the privacy promise.
  • CORS-bound webhooks.A browser cannot post where the server refuses; the UI says so.
